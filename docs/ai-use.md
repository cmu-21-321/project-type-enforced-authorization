# AI-use statement

Throughout the project, I used AI (Claude Opus 5.5) for the following:

1. Prior to scaffolding the project, I fleshed out my original idea by asking AI to search for existing literature on the topic. I settled on my two main theorems with the help of its research.
2. While getting started, I asked AI to search Mathlib for existing theorems and proofs I could use in my own formalization.
3. After sessions where I made changes to several parts of the formalization across several files, I asked AI to help stage the correct hunks for each part and suggest focused commit messages (I wasn't a fan of its writing, so I mostly didn't use these).
4. When I got stuck on a particular step, I asked AI specifically for hints that avoided revealing the entire proof structure.
5. At the end of the project, I asked AI to evaluate my Lean code for style and idiomaticity. It gave me two minor suggestions on top of the feedback posted on Canvas, and I implemented them by hand.
6. At the end of the project, I also asked it whether my examples file had sufficient coverage of the lemmas and theorems I wrote, but it suggested several things I didn't feel were necessary, so I mostly didn't include them.
